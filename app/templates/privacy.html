Privacy notice

Version {{ settings.consent_version }}

{{ settings.app_name }} helps people in Ontario find a family doctor. This notice explains what we collect, why, who sees it and when we delete it. We follow Canada's federal privacy law for businesses (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Contact details: your email, and your phone number if you give it.
  • Location: your postal code and how far you can travel.
  • About the search: who needs a doctor, an age group, household size, preferred language, how long you have been without a family doctor, whether you have an Ontario health card, and whether you are registered with Health Care Connect.
  • Optional need answers: yes/no answers about health and access to care, only if you tick them and give separate consent. We never ask for diagnoses or medications.
  • How you heard about us, if you tell us.

We do not ask for your name or health card number, and we do not store your IP address.

Why we use it

  • To find family practices within your travel distance that have room for new patients.
  • To email you about openings and about your signup.
  • To decide who hears about an opening first (see below).
  • To count demand by area so we know where to look, using totals that cannot identify you.
  • If you agree, to ask you up to twice how things went after you find a doctor.

Who sees it

We do not share your details with clinics. When a practice tells us it has room, we email you its contact details and you decide whether to contact it. We do not sell your data. Our email and hosting providers process data for us under contract. Your signup data is stored on servers in Canada. Our email provider may process your email address outside Canada in order to deliver messages.

How long we keep it

SituationWhat happens
You do not confirm your emailEverything is deleted after 7 days.
You are on the listWe keep your details while you are looking. After 11 months with no activity, we email to ask if you are still looking. If you do not answer within 30 days, we delete your contact details.
You find a doctorWe delete your contact details right away, or after 90 days if you agreed to a follow-up.
After contact details are deletedWe keep the first 3 characters of your postal code, your age group, your answers and dates for up to 24 months to measure and improve the service, then delete them. We protect this information as personal information until it is deleted.
TotalsMonthly counts by area, which cannot identify anyone, are kept.

How we decide who hears first

When a practice has room for a few patients, we tell the people who need care most first. We give points for the health and access answers you choose to share (capped, so no single answer decides), for infants and adults 75 or older, and for how long you have been without a family doctor. Everyone also gains points the longer they wait on our list, so no one is left at the bottom. Ties go to whoever signed up first.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has a link to update your signup or delete your data right away.
  • You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you by emailing {{ settings.org_contact_email }}.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time. If you withdraw consent to use your need answers, we delete them.
  • If you have a concern we cannot resolve, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
