Your privacy

We ask for as little as possible.

Here is exactly what we collect, what we never collect, and who sees it.

What we ask for

  • Your email (phone is optional)
  • Your postal code and how far you can travel
  • A few multiple-choice questions
  • Optional yes/no questions about need, only with your consent

What we never ask for

  • Your name
  • Your health card number
  • Diagnoses, medications or medical records

Who sees it

  • No clinic sees your details. You contact them yourself.
  • We never sell your data.
  • Stored on servers in Canada.
  • Delete everything with one click, any time.

Read the full privacy notice

Free for everyone

No fees. No paying to get ahead.

There is no premium tier and nothing to buy. Everyone in an area hears about openings by the same published rules, with people who need care most told first.

How it works

Three steps. Most of them are ours.

  1. 1

    You sign up

    It takes about 3 minutes. Set how far you are willing to travel, from 1 to 25 km.

  2. 2

    We reach out to practices near you

    We contact family practices within your distance and keep checking back, so you do not have to.

  3. 3

    We tell you when one has room

    We email you how to contact the practice. You apply directly, and they decide.

We will reach out to practices near you, but we cannot guarantee a match. Please also register with Health Care Connect, Ontario's free matching service. Signing up for both is a good idea.
