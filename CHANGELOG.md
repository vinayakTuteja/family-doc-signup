# Changelog

## 2026-11-01

- Signup is now one question per screen, with a progress bar, Back and Next, auto-advance on
  tap and a full-form fallback without JavaScript. Written for older phones (plain ES5 script,
  about 15 KB compressed for the whole page).
- New landing page design: intro, privacy summary, free and founder story, how it works.
- Travel distance is a 1 to 25 km slider.
- New question: would a household take spots for only some of its members.
- Ranking: added medication load, follow-up needed, no usable walk-in or virtual care, limited
  support, financial hardship, rural postal codes and a bonus for a health need combined with a
  barrier. Dropped family history, overdue screenings, life changes and navigation.

## 2026-10-31

- First version: patient signup with consent, email confirmation, manage link, daily retention,
  admin dashboard, CSV export, Fly.io config for the Toronto region.
- One-step local start scripts and a test inbox for local testing.
- Click-through preview (`preview/`).
